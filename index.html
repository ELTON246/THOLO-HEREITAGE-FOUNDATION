Welcome

Tholo Heritage Foundation is a nonprofit dedicated to preserving and revitalizing South Africa’s indigenous languages and cultural expressions—including those often overlooked, such as the wisdom of elders, oral traditions, and the needs of the Deaf community.

About Us

Our work bridges generations—empowering young people to learn from elders, documenting stories, idioms, proverbs, and rituals, and making this cultural knowledge accessible to all.

Founder: Elton Pienaar, a passionate youth trainer from Cape Town, found his calling unexpectedly in heritage work. With over two decades of experience mentoring young people, he brings vision, humility, and action to the cultural landscape. “Some things choose you,” he says.

Our Team

Our team includes language custodians, youth ambassadors, researchers, Deaf community advocates, and cultural practitioners from across South Africa. If you're passionate about heritage, there's a space for you too.

Deaf Community Inclusion

We believe in inclusive heritage. That means working with Deaf interpreters, producing signed versions of recorded oral content, and recognizing South African Sign Language as part of our living heritage.

If you're part of the Deaf community and would like to get involved or contribute, get in touch with us.
