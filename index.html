Welcome

Our mission is to document, celebrate, and preserve the indigenous languages and intangible cultural heritage of South Africa, including inclusive participation from the Deaf community through signed language initiatives.

Founded by Elton Pienaar, a lifelong youth trainer turned passionate advocate for cultural preservation, the Foundation is dedicated to making heritage accessible and vibrant for all communities.
